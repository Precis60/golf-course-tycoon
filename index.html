    How to Play

    1) Design & Build

    Select a paint tool and drag on the canvas. Tee and Green use the selected Hole No. (1–9) — each hole needs its matching tee and green. Facilities occupy a 2×2 footprint and add revenue, prestige or condition benefits. Bulldoze removes tiles/facilities. Pan/zoom to work on detail.

    2) Manage

    Balance green fee, maintenance, marketing and staff. Advance Week for a full P&L: green fees, memberships, Pro Shop and Restaurant income versus wages, per-tile mowing, facility upkeep, marketing and loan interest. Random events and weather change demand. Win at Week 12 with $850k+, Condition 70+, Reputation 65+.

    3) Play

    Every hole is drawn from your actual tee, green, fairways and hazards. Drag to aim, pick Driver / 5-Iron / Wedge / Putter, hold Swing and release. Water = penalty drop, sand and rough reduce power, trees can deflect. On the green, watch the break indicator. Finish the round to bank prize money into Manage.

    Tips: connected fairways, water and trees near (not on) the line of play, and a Practice Range + Clubhouse lift rating fastest. Keep maintenance funded — Condition drives everything.
